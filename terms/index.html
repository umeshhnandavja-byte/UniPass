1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering an account and submitting documents through the UniPass platform, you agree to abide by these Terms and Conditions. UniPass acts as an automated intake and processing engine for university Designated School Officers (DSOs) and is powered by the NewgenONE platform.

2. AI Document Processing & Identity Verification

You acknowledge and consent that UniPass utilizes Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to parse and verify the documents you upload. Specifically:

  • Passport Data: Your Passport's Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) will be scanned to establish a legal identity anchor, overriding any conflicting name orders entered manually.
  • Financial Records: Bank statements will be parsed to evaluate multi-currency liquid funds against institutional tuition requirements.
  • Academic Transcripts: Foreign grading systems will be automatically converted to standardized GPA scales.

3. User Responsibilities & Fraud Prevention

You agree to submit only genuine, unaltered, and legally valid documentation. Any attempt to upload forged bank statements, manipulated transcripts, or falsified passports will result in immediate account termination and automatic flagging within institutional compliance systems.

4. Disclaimer of Visa Guarantee

UniPass is a document orchestration and verification tool designed to generate compliance-ready payloads (such as US SEVIS JSON, UK CAS, and Australian eCoE). UniPass does not issue visas. Final visa sponsorship decisions remain at the sole discretion of the host university (DSO), and final visa issuance is strictly governed by the respective national embassy or consulate.

5. Data Privacy & Storage (NewgenONE ECM)

Your sensitive personal and financial documents are stored securely using NewgenONE Contextual Content Services (ECM). Documents are retained only as long as required for immigration audit purposes and are strictly role-gated to authorized university compliance officers.
